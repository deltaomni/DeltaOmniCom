Safety Cross · Launching

The month's safety, at a glance.

The digital Safety Cross. Each day of the month takes the color of its most serious recorded event, and the whole team reads the pattern without explanation.

It works on its own. Coupled to the 3D map, it completes the governance of the DeltaOmni platform.

What it does

A pattern everyone reads

Green for zero accidents; yellow, black and hatched for each level of severity or missing information. Days the month doesn't have show in gray.

Recorded in seconds

The day's entry is made on a phone, by whoever is in the area. The cross updates at once, on the board and on screen.

The core indicators

Days without accidents, events by type and month-over-month trends, ready for the safety meeting.
