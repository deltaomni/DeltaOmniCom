Applications

Wherever a record is made under time pressure, or weighs on safety, there is an application. In other words: almost everywhere at work.

Gate

Gate receiving

Incoming vehicles, materials and open issues recorded at the gate, without a clipboard.

“Coil trailer arrived without an invoice. Waiting in the yard.”

Logistics

Stock count

The count is spoken aisle by aisle, hands free to check the material.

“Warehouse C, position twelve: eight bags of polypropylene, one damaged.”

Quality

Non-conformance on the line

The deviation is recorded at the workstation, on the spot, with location and batch, before the part moves on.

“Morning batch with burrs on the drilling. Quarantined.”

EHS

Unsafe conditions and near misses

The base of the safety pyramid gets recorded, and the map shows where risk builds up.

“Converter two ladle siren is not working.”

Maintenance

Equipment downtime

Downtime opens with a start time and only closes when the machine runs again. MTTR comes straight from the data.

“Cell four down, limit switch, starting maintenance.”

Communication

Radio channel

What is said on the radio becomes a record, instead of being lost between shifts.

“Vehicle LV33, driver CFE36 here, right of way granted.”

And everything that has no channel today. Any relevant change without a form, system or owner can be spoken, recorded and found later, right where it happened.

What it delivers

Recorded in seconds

One sentence is enough. Shift, operator, time and location are added automatically.

No event left out

Near misses, small deviations and short stops get recorded, and the data shows the trend before a serious event.

Ready for your systems

Every record is ready to be exported via API to the systems the plant already runs (A3, 4D, 8D…).

Frequently asked questions

What is Events Scanner?

It is MapOmega's voice-driven event scanner. The operator describes an event by speaking, AI classifies area, type and location, and the record goes into a structured database and onto the 3D map of the plant.

Where does it apply?

In any record made under time pressure or that affects safety: non-conformances, unsafe conditions, downtime, radio communications, stock counts, gate receiving, and any relevant change that has no reporting channel today.

Do we need to install an app?

No. Events are logged from the phone's web browser. Whoever sees the problem opens the page, speaks and, if useful, attaches a photo.

Does it work in noisy environments?

Yes, within known limits. During rollout we map the noise level of each zone and recommend the right capture mode: speaking directly into the phone, or an external microphone in the noisiest areas.

Does it integrate with the systems we already use?

Yes. Every record is structured and can be sent by API to the plant's systems, including formatted as A3, 4D or 8D.
