Events Scanner

The voice-driven event scanner. Shop-floor events recorded in seconds, straight onto the 3D map of your plant.

The first person to see a problem is the one on the line, hands busy. With Events Scanner, they say what they saw, and the sentence becomes a structured, classified and located record. A photo can go with it when it helps. Response starts the same minute, and every record feeds one database for Quality, EHS, Production, Maintenance and Logistics.
